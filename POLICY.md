# Policy

- Educational only: scores, risks, runbooks, math, read-only `get_account`
- **Never** collect or display private keys / seeds
- Prefer multi-weight authorities over sharing full seeds with “helpers”
- Single-helper full `owner` control is always a **high** risk flag
- Cloud Wallet product recovery is not a substitute for on-chain authority design
- Live authority changes belong in multi-sig / hardware blueprints with WharfKit
